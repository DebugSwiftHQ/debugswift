# debugswift.com changelog

What changed on the website, newest first. Written from the history of its private source.

## October 2026

- Lead Engine page rewritten to match what version 2 actually does.
- Analytics that set no cookies, so there is still no cookie banner.
- Privacy policy updated for the new hosting.
- Security update to the framework underneath the site.

## September 2026

- Moved hosting to Cloudflare.
- **Text no longer jumps when the font loads.** The fallback font is measured to match Satoshi's widths, and an automated check keeps it that way.
- A Tools menu in the header.
- Faster pages: the next page is fetched when you hover a link, with no render-blocking stylesheet.
- No em dashes anywhere a visitor reads, enforced by an automated check.
- Security headers on every response.
- An automated check that walks every page at desktop and phone widths: contrast, colour use, layout overflow, headings and metadata.
- Privacy policy corrected: it named a company that never touches your data.
- How the Lead Engine works, folded into its own page.

## August 2026

- DuctForge featured on the Custom Web Apps & SaaS page.
- The blog and the free tools moved to their own deployments, served under debugswift.com.
- The leak calculator keeps the number a visitor just worked out.

## July 2026: the rebuild

- **Repositioned from a solo studio to a founder-led agency,** and from one product to the whole philosophy: understand a business, find what costs it most, fix it with technology.
- Canonical addresses, www redirected to the main domain, and tighter page descriptions.
- The diagnosis form never shows "thanks" for an enquiry that didn't arrive. If sending fails, it hands you WhatsApp and the email address instead.
- Real photos and a redrawn chat mockup.
- Full rebuild: home, Lead Engine, a services hub and a page per service, about, contact, sitemap, 404.
- A bespoke design system: cream, ink, indigo and a rationed clay, set in Satoshi, with Deb the swift.

## Before 2026

- November 2024: first single-page site.
- May 2025: complete rebuild.
